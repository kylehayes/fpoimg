Quick start

Use a URL directly in your markup. This creates a 1200 × 630 gradient placeholder with a caption:

<img src="https://fpoimg.com/1200x630?gradient=ocean&amp;text=Blog+Cover&amp;dims=false"
     width="1200" height="630" alt="Blog cover placeholder">

Omit the extension for PNG. For other formats use URLs such as /400x300.webp?text=Card or /400x300.svg?gradient=sunset.
